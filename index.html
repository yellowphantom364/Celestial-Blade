MECHANICS // FRAME DATA

BLADE, POGO & SOUL ECONOMY

Inspired by Hollow Knight and Celeste, movement and combat share a unified physics body. Every strike harvests Soul energy and alters momentum—whether recoiling upward off a spike bed or parrying an astral orb mid-flight.

Health never auto-refills between rooms. Clearing a stage carries your exact HP and Soul gauge forward until you channel Focus or reach an Attunement Shrine.

SYS.01 // MELEE 8F ACTIVE

3-HIT SOLAR COMBO

Chain three rapid slashes within a 28-frame combo window. The third swing unleashes a forward-lunging Solar Finisher that deals 2× damage (2 HP) with expanded reach.

COOLDOWN: 10F · FINISHER LUNGE: 2.1 PX/F
SYS.02 // AERIAL -3.58 VY

POGO & DASH RESET

Hold Down + Slash in mid-air to strike beneath you. Connecting with any enemy, boss, or crystal spike launches you upward and immediately refreshes your air dash.

RECOIL I-FRAMES: 12F · AIR DASH RESET: INSTANT
SYS.03 // DEFENSE 14F I-FRAMES

PROJECTILE ORB PARRY

Slash incoming Void Mage, Astral Construct, or Seraph Vael orbs to shatter them on contact, granting bonus Soul (+16 to +22) and 14 frames of invulnerability.

SOUL GAIN: +16 ENEMY / +22 BOSS ORB
SYS.04 // MOBILITY 9F BURST

ASTRAL DASH & COYOTE

Burst horizontally at 4.35 px/frame while phasing through enemy bodies and projectiles. Assisted by 6-frame coyote time, 6-frame jump buffering, and 3px corner correction.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE // NOTES

ENGINE DISCLOSURES

Every subsystem in Celestial Blade—from sub-pixel AABB collision and verlet scarf physics to the 4-track procedural audio synthesizer—is written in plain JavaScript without external runtime libraries.

01 // DETERMINISTIC 60 FPS TIMESTEP & SUB-PIXEL PHYSICS
Game.js runs an accumulator loop locked to VIEWPORT.STEP_MS (16.6667ms) so jump arcs, 8-frame slash windows, and hitstop pauses behave identically on 60 Hz, 120 Hz, and 144 Hz displays. PhysicsBody.js tracks fractional sub-pixel remainders (remX, remY) with 3-pixel upward corner correction so the knight never snags on ceiling ledges during high-speed aerial pogo chains.
02 // PROCEDURAL WEB AUDIO SYNTHESIS (ZERO AUDIO FILES)
SoundEngine.js synthesizes all four biome musical scores (Exploration, Bastion, Labyrinth, and Boss) plus 18 distinct combat sound effects in real time using native Web Audio API triangle, square, sawtooth, and filtered noise nodes. When the player takes fatal damage, music ducks automatically while the crystal heart cracks and shatters.
03 // CUSTOM PIXEL BITMAP FONT & VERLET SCARF RENDERING
To guarantee crisp 1:1 pixel typography at 320×180 without browser font anti-aliasing blur, PixelFont.js encodes a complete 5×5 bitmap glyph set drawn directly to the canvas context. The knight’s crimson scarf is simulated via a 5-node trailing chain that reacts dynamically to facing direction, dash velocity, and wind oscillation.
04 // LOCAL EXECUTION, BUNDLER & ZERO-SERVER FILE MODE
During development, index.html loads native ES modules from ./src/main.js (run npm run dev for http://localhost:8080). For standalone offline play, node scripts/build.js concatenates all 19 modules in dependency order into ./dist/bundle.js, allowing index.html to run directly over the file:// protocol when double-clicked.
